## Multi-company, multi-currency accounting for your desktop

Ledgerik is a full double-entry accounting workspace for Windows, macOS and Linux, built for people who keep books for more than one company — and in more than one currency — without handing their ledgers to a hosted service.

Each company is its own `.acw` company file: a local SQLite database that you create, open and back up yourself. Switch companies by opening a different file, and keep every journal, invoice and report where you can see it.

## The books, done properly

- **General ledger and chart of accounts** — journal entries with debit and credit lines, opening balances, journal templates, transfers and a company setup that starts you from a clean, balanced set of books.
- **Journals are append-only.** Posted entries are never edited or deleted: a correction voids the original and posts a reversing entry, so the audit trail stays intact.
- **Fiscal periods and year end** — close a fiscal year with a controlled closing entry and carry the balances forward.
- **Multi-currency** — company and foreign currencies, exchange rates, and realized exchange gain/loss postings when a payment settles at a different rate.

## Invoicing, expenses and banking

- Invoices, estimates, credit notes and recurring invoices, with payment reminders, email settings and a client portal.
- Expenses with a receipt gallery, categorization rules and mileage tracking, plus 1099-NEC reporting.
- Bank accounts and payments recorded against invoices, with Stripe payments and client statements.

## Reports that reconcile

Trial balance, balance sheet, income statement, cash flow, A/R aging, budget vs. actual and tax return reports, all built from the same ledger and exportable to PDF, CSV and XLSX.

## A desktop workspace, not a web form

Every screen opens as a floating, draggable, resizable panel on a canvas you arrange yourself. A command palette and keyboard shortcuts get you anywhere, and projects and time tracking live right beside the books.

## Free where it matters, paid once for the rest

**Ledgerik Basic is free forever**: the general ledger, your master data, financial statements, backups and file access. **Ledgerik Pro** is a one-time purchase that unlocks client billing, expenses, banking, time tracking, projects, contacts, the audit log, tax returns and CSV import. No subscription, no ads.

Available in English, German, Spanish and French.
