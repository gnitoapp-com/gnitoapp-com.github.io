Welcome to the Ledgerik user manual. Ledgerik keeps each company's books in its own `.acw` company file on your computer; this manual walks through setting up a company, keeping the books, and getting reports out.

The manual is being written in stages. The sections below form the structure — each one is a stub for now and will be filled in as it is completed.
