Ledgerik has two plans. There is no trial, no subscription and no per-seat pricing: one lifetime purchase moves an installation from Basic to Pro.

## Basic and What Pro Adds

**Ledgerik Basic is free forever.** Its own words: *"The general ledger, your master data, financial statements, backups and file access are free forever."* In practice that is the chart of accounts, sales tax codes, currencies, journals and journal templates, opening balances and the year-end close. It is also the three financial statements, and the whole backups window: snapshots, restore and encrypted import.

**Ledgerik Pro** unlocks *"client billing, expenses, banking, time tracking, projects, contacts, the audit log, tax returns and CSV import"* — one lifetime purchase, and every feature is unlocked for life.

Concretely, 22 of the application's windows are free and 30 are Pro. A window is either open to you or not; there is no partial feature.

**Your data is never locked.** Opening a company file, reading and writing the books is free on every plan, and a Pro feature running out of licence never hides an existing record — it stops you opening that window.

## Opening a Pro Feature on Basic

Open one and the window stops at a gate: **"… is part of Ledgerik Pro"**, with the upsell text and **Buy Ledgerik Pro**. Nothing is created, nothing is changed; closing the gate returns you to what you were doing.

## Activating a Licence

**Help ▸ License**, or the **License** tab of **App Settings**, opens the activation dialog.

1. Buy Pro in the browser. The purchase page issues the key when it completes.
2. Paste the key into **License Key** and press **Activate**. Ledgerik reports *"License activated — Ledgerik Pro."*
3. Once activated, the dialog shows **Status**, **Issued** and **Last Validated**.

**Save the key.** The dialog says so, and it is not exaggeration: *"Use it when you reinstall or install the app on another computer that you own."* The key is not stored anywhere you can retrieve it from.

**Deactivate License** returns this installation to Basic. The data stays.

## How Validation Works

Activation checks the key with the purchase service and caches the result. On **every launch** a cached Pro licence is re-checked, so a refund or revocation takes effect at the next start.

**Offline, Ledgerik stays on Pro.** When the check cannot reach the service, the cached state is kept — the application is offline-first and does not punish you for flying.

Three failures are reported rather than guessed at: *"Invalid license key. Please check and try again."*, *"Could not reach the purchase service. Check your internet connection."*, and *"No purchase product is configured. Please contact support."*

## The 30-Day Guarantee

Pro comes with a **30-day money-back guarantee**: ask within 30 days of purchase, and access returns to Basic. **Nothing is removed from your company files**, and the guarantee applies once per customer. Once you have bought, the paywall shows the date your refund window closes.

The refund policy itself is described in [Refund Policy](/ledgerik/manual/refund_policy/) — the page the paywall's *"Read the refund policy"* link opens.

## What a Licence Does Not Do

- It does not change your data, your company file or your reports — it changes which windows open.
- It does not expire. A licence bought once stays active for that installation, subject to the validation above.
- It is not transferable per user: the key is what moves between machines you own, not an account.
