A journal template is the lines of an entry, stored for reuse. It is not an entry: it has no date, no amounts in the books and no effect until you use it.

**Journals ▸ List Journal Templates** lists them.

## Saving one

Write the entry in **Create Journal Entry** and choose **Save as a template** instead of Save. Ledgerik confirms with *"The journal has been saved as a template"*, and the lines are stored as they stand — accounts, sides, memos and sales tax codes.

A template keeps the shape of the entry. It does not keep the date, so the day you use it is the day it belongs to.

## Using one

Open a template and choose **Use this journal template**. Ledgerik opens the entry form with the lines already in place.

Check them before you save. The date is empty, so you choose the day the entry belongs to. The description, the type, the currency and the lines are already there, and **Save** still waits for the balance to reach zero.

Nothing is posted by using a template. Only saving the entry posts it, exactly as if you had typed the lines yourself.

## Deleting one

**Delete** asks for confirmation, then removes the template. Entries already posted from it are untouched — a template is a form, not a record of what was posted.

## What a template is not

- Not a recurring entry. Nothing generates or posts from a template on a schedule.
- Not a saved draft. A template never appears in **List Journals** and never carries a badge.
- Not a correction. Voiding is the only way to correct a posted entry — see [Journal Entries](/ledgerik/manual/journal-entries/).
