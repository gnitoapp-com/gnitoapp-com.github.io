Ledgerik Pro is sold with a **30-day money-back guarantee**.

## What the guarantee covers

- It covers **Ledgerik Pro** — the lifetime purchase, not a subscription, because there is no subscription.
- It runs for **30 days from the date of purchase**.
- It applies **once per customer**.

If you bought Pro and the application shows your licence, it also shows the date your refund window closes. Nothing needs to be calculated by hand.

## What a refund does

- **Your access returns to Ledgerik Basic.** Basic is free forever, and everything the sale page lists as Basic stays available.
- **Nothing is removed from your company files.** Not a journal, not an invoice, not a receipt, not a setting. The books you have written are yours and remain complete — see [Company Files](/ledgerik/manual/company-files/).
- **Your existing records stay readable.** A Pro window closing does not hide data you already entered; it stops you opening that window again — see [Licensing](/ledgerik/manual/licensing/).

## How to ask

Use the [support page](/support/) and say you are asking for a refund under the 30-day guarantee. Include the email address the purchase was made with and your licence key, so the purchase can be found.

The licence key is the one the purchase issued and the activation dialog asked you to save. If you no longer have it, say so in your message.

## What the guarantee is not

- It is not a trial. Ledgerik has no trial period, and nothing is charged on a schedule — so a refund is a return of a single payment, not the cancellation of a plan.
- It is not a limit on the software. A refund does not deactivate your company files, and Basic remains a working accounting application.
- It is not affected by how much you have used the application.

## Where the purchase itself happens

Prices, the purchase page and the store listings are on the [Ledgerik page](/ledgerik/). This page only describes the guarantee.
