Across the top of the Ledgerik window are three menus — **File**, **Windows** and **Help** — and the fourth item of the File menu, **App Settings**, opens the preferences. Everything here is described in the order it appears.

## File

- **New** — asks for a name and a folder, and creates a new company file. [Company Files](/ledgerik/manual/company-files/) has the procedure.
- **Open** — opens an existing company file.
- **Close** — closes the open company. The file stays where it is; nothing is deleted.
- **App Settings** — opens the preferences window described below.
- **Exit** — quits Ledgerik.

There is no recent-files list, no print command and no import or export here. Importing lives under **Company Settings ▸ Import Data**, and printing belongs to the window you are in.

## Windows

- **Stack Windows** — tidies the windows you have opened back into place on the canvas.
- **Close All** — closes every open window at once. Your data is not affected; only the windows close.

Both entries are greyed out while no window is open. The canvas itself — opening, moving, resizing and closing a window — is covered in [Getting Started](/ledgerik/manual/getting-started/).

## Help

- **User Help** — opens this manual in your browser. The manual is the application's help: there is nothing else to install to read it.
- **Support** — opens the support page.
- **License** — opens the licence dialog, where a key is entered or a licence released. [Licensing](/ledgerik/manual/licensing/) covers activation.
- **About** — shows the application name and icon, the copyright line with the current year, and buttons for the **Terms of Service** and the **Privacy Policy**.

## App Settings

**App Settings** opens a window with four tabs: **Date**, **Appearance**, **Language** and **License**. Every change here is saved as soon as you make it, takes effect immediately, and belongs to this installation rather than to the company you have open.

### Date

- **App Date** — the date Ledgerik works from, which can differ from the system date. Choose it deliberately: the home screen shows it, and dates that default into new records come from it.
- **Date Format** — the format used for entering and displaying dates throughout the application.

### Appearance

- **Dark Mode** — switches between the dark and light appearance of the whole application.
- **Color Palette** — **Vibrant**, **Pastel**, **Neutral** or **Monochrome**.
- **Seed color** — the colour the palette is built from. Pick a new one and every accent in the application follows.
- **Number Format** — how numbers are displayed and parsed: thousands separators and the decimal mark.

### Language

- **System** — follow the language of the operating system.
- **English**, **German**, **French**, **Spanish** — choose one explicitly.

Switching language applies at once, without a restart. Anything not yet translated remains in English.

### License

The tab shows the licence **Status**, the **License Key**, when the licence was **Issued** and when it was **Last Validated**, with a **Manage License** button. On Ledgerik Basic the tab instead offers the upgrade, and **Manage License** is where a key is entered — see [Licensing](/ledgerik/manual/licensing/).

## Why these settings do not travel

Everything in App Settings belongs to the installation: the date pattern, the language, the colours and the number format live on this computer, not inside the company file. Open the same company on another machine and your appearance choices stay behind, while the books arrive intact. The settings that do belong to the company — its name, fiscal year, base currency and accounts — are under **Company Settings**, described in [Company Settings](/ledgerik/manual/settings/).
