## Conquer the Cards in Imperial Style!

Step into the grandeur of ancient Rome with Romanitaire, the card game that blends classic Klondike Solitaire with an epic Roman twist! Whether you're a seasoned strategist or a casual player, immerse yourself in a world of emperors and stack your way to victory.

## Why Choose Roman Solitaire?

**Handedness-Friendly Controls**: Seamlessly switch between left-handed and right-handed modes, ensuring comfortable gameplay no matter your preference. Drag, tap, and flip cards with ease—designed for one-handed mastery!

**Flexible Orientations**: Play in portrait for on-the-go sessions or landscape.

**Unlimited Undos**!

**Customization Options**: Tailor your game in the settings menu—adjust draw rules (1-card or 3-card).

**No ads** interrupt your conquests—just pure, addictive Solitaire fun.

Whether you're unwinding after a long day or honing your skills for glory, Romanitaire delivers endless entertainment with a historical edge.
